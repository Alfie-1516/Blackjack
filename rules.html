Poker

#1

Objective of the Game

The goal of poker is to win chips or money
Either have the best hand or make opponents fold.

The primary objective of poker is to accumulate chips or money by outplaying your opponents through strategy and skill. This can be achieved in two main ways: either by holding the strongest hand at showdown—when all remaining players reveal their cards—or by successfully bluffing or betting in a way that persuades your opponents to fold before the showdown. In essence, poker combines elements of probability, psychology, and risk management to outsmart other players and win the pot

#2

Basic Poker Hand Rankings (Best to Worst)

  • Royal Flush A, K, Q, J, 10 all same suit
  • Straight Flush: Five cards in sequence & same suit
  • Four of a Kind: Four cards of the same rank
  • Full House: Three of a kind plus a pair
  • Flush: Five cards same suit, any order
  • Straight: Five cards in sequence, different suits
  • Three of a Kind: Three cards of the same rank
  • Two Pair: Two separate pairs
  • One Pair: Two cards of the same rank
  • High Card: If no combination, highest card wins

#3

Gameplay Overview

Poker is played in rounds where players are dealt cards, place bets, and try to win the pot by having the best hand or making others fold.

  1. Players are dealt two private cards (known as "hole cards").
  2. Five community cards are dealt face-up in three stages:
    • Flop – 3 cards
    • Turn – 1 card
    • River – 1 card
  3. Players use a combination of their hole cards and the community cards to make the best five-card hand.
  4. Betting rounds occur after each deal of cards.

#4

Common Poker Terms

Understanding poker terminology is crucial for effective communication and strategy in the game.
Here are some common terms you should know:

  • Check – Decline to bet but keep your cards.
  • Bet – Wager chips or money into the pot.
  • Call – Match another player’s bet.
  • Raise – Increase the previous bet.
  • Fold – Forfeit the hand and any chance at winning the pot.
  • Blinds – Forced bets to start the action (small blind, big blind).
  • All-In – Bet all the chips you have.

#5

Basic Poker Etiquette

Good poker etiquette ensures a smooth and enjoyable game for everyone.
Here are some key points to remember:

  • Always act in turn.
  • Do not reveal your cards while the hand is ongoing.
  • Be respectful to all players.
  • Handle your chips neatly and clearly.
  • Announce your moves when necessary (especially "raise" or "all-in").
